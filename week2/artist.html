Pewdiepie

Felix Arvid Ulf Kjellberg, widely known online as PewDiePie, is a Swedish YouTuber, streamer, and internet personality who became one of the most influential figures in digital culture. Born on October 24, 1989, in Gothenburg, Sweden, he originally pursued a degree in industrial economics and technology management but dropped out to focus on his burgeoning YouTube career, famously funding his early videos by working at a hot dog stand.He created his PewDiePie channel in 2010, initially focusing on "Let's Play" gaming commentaries, particularly horror games like Amnesia: The Dark Descent. His energetic, unfiltered, and highly comedic reactions quickly attracted a massive global audience. By August 2013, he became the most-subscribed user on YouTube, a position he held for several years. Over the decade, his content diversified into vlogs, meme reviews, and cultural commentary. Today, he lives a relatively quiet life in Japan with his family, having transitioned into a semi-retired state while remaining a legendary icon of internet history.
